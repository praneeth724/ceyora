Our story

Born on this island, built for travellers

CEYORA TRAVELS began with three friends, one van and a belief that the best way to see Sri Lanka is through the people who call it home. Twelve years later we are a team of guides, drivers, naturalists and planners spread across the island.

We keep our groups small, our itineraries flexible and our partners local. Every stay, every meal and every guide is chosen because we would send our own family there.
